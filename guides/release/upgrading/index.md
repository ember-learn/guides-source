When someone says they are "upgrading" their Ember app, it could mean one of several things:

- Upgrading the **Ember version**, for instance from `ember-source` v5 to `ember-source` v6.
- Upgrading the [**Ember edition**](https://emberjs.com/editions/), like implementing parts of Octane or Polaris. For instance:
	- Upgrading components to **Glimmer components**. (Octane)
	- Upgrading **template compilation** by introducing GJS format. (Polaris)
	- Upgrading the **build system** from EmberCLI-Broccoli to Embroider-Vite. (Polaris)
    - etc
- Upgrading the historical **data layer** by migrating from EmberData to WarpDrive (only for apps including it in the first place).

Each type of upgrade matches one aspect of Ember evolution over the years – the framework, the underlying programming model, the ecosystem – that keeps your app modern and aligned with the latest web standards.
